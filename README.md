# 📊 Seoudi Supermarket - Customer & Sales Performance Dashboard
# 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze transaction performance, revenue streams, and customer demographics for Seoudi Supermarket. By modeling key metrics across purchasing channels, product lines, and demographic segments, this dashboard delivers actionable insights to optimize marketing, targeted promotions, and customer retention strategies.

# 📈 Executive Summary & High-Level KPIs
Across the analyzed dataset, Seoudi Supermarket achieved:

- 💰 Total Revenue: $275,064

- 👥 Total Unique Customers: 1,000

- 🧺 Average Orders per Customer: 6.95

- ⭐ Average Customer Rating: 5.40 / 10

- 💎 Customer Loyalty Rate: 49%

# 🔍 Strategic Dashboard Insights
# 1. 🛒 Sales & Channel Performance (Executive Overview)
- 📉 Monthly Trends: Sales peaked early in the year (March reaching $28,617), followed by mid-year stability and a seasonal low in December ($17,857).

- Mixed Channel Dominance: Customers utilizing a Mixed Channel (In-Store + Online) generated the highest total revenue ($95,164), closely followed by Online-only ($91,604) and In-Store ($88,295).

- 🏷️ Discount Impact: 48% of purchases involved a discount, indicating high responsiveness to promotional pricing.

- 🏆 Top Revenue Categories: Jewelry & Accessories ($15,139) and Sports & Outdoors ($14,611) led product revenue, followed by Electronics ($13,842).

# 2. 🎯 Customer Segmentation Analysis (Customer Segment)
- 🎂 Age Distribution: The 36–44 age bracket represents the largest single customer cohort (287 customers), followed by ages 27–35 (274) and 18–26 (256).

- 💳 Income Tier: High-income customers generate significantly higher sales volume (515 customers) compared to middle-income tiers (485 customers).

- 🎓 Education Breakdown: Bachelor's degree holders form the largest education demographic (341 customers), followed by High School graduates (331) and Master's degree holders (328).

- 🛍️ Gender & Category Preferences: Female customers drive higher purchase volume across key categories such as Jewelry & Accessories (29 vs. 17) and Electronics (28 vs. 20).

 # 💡 Strategic Recommendations
- 🔄 Capitalize on Multi-Channel Shoppers: Promote omni-channel loyalty incentives, as mixed-channel shoppers represent the highest overall value.

- 🚀 End-of-Year Revenue Push: Implement targeted year-end promotional campaigns to boost December sales performance.

- 🎯 Target Core Demographics: Direct premium product lines (Jewelry, Sports, Electronics) toward high-income customers aged 27–44.

# 🛠️ Tools & Features Used
- 🟢 Microsoft Excel: Data Cleaning, Power Query, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Dynamic UI/UX Navigation.

- 📊 Data Visualization: Custom KPI Cards, Segmented Bar Charts, Line Charts, and Stacked Distribution Views.
